ABOUT

KEVIN
CORNEJO.

I’m a Brooklyn-raised interaction design student with a background in graphic design. New York shapes how I see design, and it shows up all over this site.

I’m working toward a B.S. in Interaction Design at Farmingdale State College after studying graphic design at Nassau Community College. Graphic design gave me layout, type and branding. Interaction design adds research, prototyping and testing.

Before design school, I spent seven years as vice president of a home care company, managing a team of about 15. It taught me to communicate clearly, manage deadlines and keep work organized.

I’m most drawn to game UI. I play a lot, Pokémon especially, and I’m learning to design and build interfaces for games. Away from the screen, I’m usually taking photos.
