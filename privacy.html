PRIVACY.

Effective November 24, 2026

Who runs this site

offaxis-creative.com is the personal portfolio of Kevin Cornejo (OFF/AXIS), based in New York. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, an optional subject, and your message. I use it to read and reply to your message, and for nothing else. I don’t sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it

Form messages are delivered by Formspree, which stores the submission and forwards it to my email. Formspree handles it under its own privacy policy, linked from its website.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics, ad pixels or other tracking. Links to LinkedIn take you to that site, which has its own policy. The home page asks Open-Meteo for the current New York weather so the skyline can show rain or snow; that request comes from your browser, so Open-Meteo sees your IP address, but nothing about you is sent with it. Hidden achievements you find on the site are saved only in your own browser and never sent anywhere.

How long I keep it

I keep messages as long as needed to reply and follow up, and delete them when they’re no longer needed. You can ask me to delete your message at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, using the contact form.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
